How to request deletion

  1. Email ongamemall2675@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion request".
  2. Include your Instagram username or Facebook name, and the post you commented on (if any).
  3. We delete all data related to you within 30 days and reply to confirm.

Remove the app's access yourself

  • Facebook: Settings & privacy > Settings > Business integrations (or Apps and websites) > sns-upload > Remove.
  • Instagram: Settings > Website permissions / Apps and websites > sns-upload > Remove.
