2. Information we collect from TikTok

When you connect a TikTok account, we receive only:

  • Basic profile information (user.info.basic): open ID, display name, and avatar, used to show which account is connected.
  • Access and refresh tokens, used only to upload and publish videos you choose (video.upload, video.publish).

We do not collect your TikTok password, followers, messages, viewing history, or any other data.

3. How we use it

Only to (a) show the connected account and (b) upload and publish the videos you select to that account. We do not use the data for advertising, profiling, or sale.

4. Sharing

We do not sell or share your information with third parties, except TikTok itself (to perform the upload) or when required by law.

5. Storage and security

Tokens are stored securely with restricted access and are used only by the Service. Video files are kept only as long as needed to complete the upload.

6. Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account is connected. If you disconnect the app or ask us to delete your data, we delete stored tokens and profile information within 30 days. You can also revoke access in TikTok: Settings and privacy > Security > Apps and services.

7. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, or delete your data by emailing ongamemall2675@gmail.com.
